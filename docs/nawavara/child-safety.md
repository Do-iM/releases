# Nawavara Child Safety Standards

- Effective date: November 21, 2026

This is an English translation of the Korean original ([한국어](ko/child-safety)). If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

Nawavara is a neighborhood meetup app operated by DDD. We do not tolerate child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), anywhere in the service.

## Who can use Nawavara

- You must be at least 14 years old to sign up. An account found to have given a false birth date to get around this is deleted.

## What is prohibited

Users must not post, send, request, or share any of the following in profiles, meetups, posts, comments, or chats:

1. sexual images, videos, or text involving anyone under 18 (CSAM), including drawn, edited, or AI-generated material;
2. grooming: contacting or befriending a minor in order to sexually abuse or exploit them, or moving them to another service or meeting them for that purpose;
3. sexual solicitation of, or sexual conversation with, a minor;
4. sextortion: threatening a minor or demanding money, images, or meetings using sexual images or information;
5. trafficking, sale, or commercial sexual exploitation of minors;
6. any other content or behavior that sexualizes or endangers minors.

These rules are part of our [Terms of Service](terms) (Article 7, "approach minors inappropriately"; "obscene material").

## How to report

- **In the app:** open the menu on a profile, meetup, post, or comment, or long-press a chat message, choose **Report**, and select **Sexual content**. Add a short note if you can. The report saves what was on screen at that time, so it remains for review even if the content is later deleted.
- **By email:** contact@dee3.me. Please include the user's name, the meetup or post, and when it happened. Do not attach the abusive images themselves.
- **If a child is in immediate danger,** call the police first (112 in Korea, or your local emergency number).

## What we do

1. We review reports about minors before other reports.
2. We remove the content and permanently suspend the account when we confirm a violation. Suspended users may not create a new account.
3. We keep the records needed for an investigation and report CSAM and child exploitation to the authorities, as required by the Act on the Protection of Children and Youth against Sex Offenses of the Republic of Korea: the Korean National Police Agency, and the Korea Communications Standards Commission. Where it involves another country, we also report to the relevant authority, such as the NCMEC CyberTipline.
4. We cooperate with law enforcement requests made under the law.
