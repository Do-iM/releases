# Nawavara Support

[한국어](ko/support)

## Contact us

- **In the app:** Settings > Talk to the developer > "Contact us · Suggestions" or "Report a bug". Replies arrive as a notification and appear in "My messages and replies".
- **By email:** contact@dee3.me

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I sign in?**
With your Google or Apple account. You must be at least 14 years old to sign up.

**Nearby meetups don't show up.**
Nawavara uses your location to show your neighborhood. Allow location for Nawavara in your phone's settings. If location is off, the app uses your last known location.

**I'm not getting notifications.**
Check that "Get notifications" is on in Settings > Notifications, and that notifications for Nawavara are allowed in your phone's settings.

**I bought coins but didn't get them.**
They are usually added automatically the next time you open the app. If they still don't show up, contact us with the time of purchase.

**Can I get a refund for coins?**
Unused coins can be refunded within 7 days of purchase through the store you paid with (Google Play or the App Store). Coins that have been partly used are not refundable. See Article 10 of the [Terms of Service](terms).

**How do I report or block someone?**
Open the menu on a profile, meetup, post, or comment, or long-press a chat message, then choose Report. To block someone, choose Block in the menu on their profile or post. You can unblock people in Settings > Blocked users.

**How do I delete my account?**
- Settings > Delete account > "Delete Nawavara account" deletes your Nawavara data only.
- [Delete entire account](../account/) deletes your data in all of our apps and your sign-in account. It is also in Settings > Delete account, and works even after you remove the app.
